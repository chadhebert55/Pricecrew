---
name: Subpanel catalog ratings
description: How equipment ratings and feeder protection map to Addition subpanel catalog rows.
---

A verified 100A-rated main-lug-only load center may be used for either the 60A or 100A Addition subpanel option when protected by the matching feeder breaker. Do not relabel lower-rated equipment as a higher-rated subpanel.

**Why:** The load center rating is its maximum permitted rating; the upstream feeder breaker determines whether the installation is supplied at 60A or 100A.

**How to apply:** Keep separate 60A and 100A estimating rows so quote scope remains explicit, but tie both only to source-verified equipment rated for at least the selected feeder amperage. Keep feeder conductors and breakers size-specific.
